💡 HINTS — THE 5-INGREDIENT BLUEPRINT

One chunk = one idea. Open only what you need.

🧑‍🚀 NEVER USED AI BEFORE?

You can't break anything — Claude only reads and writes text. A weak answer is feedback: change one line, go again.

🔑 THE ONE RULE

Claude's answer is only as good as your instruction — source, timeframe, grouping, constraint, exclusion.

🔍 SOURCE — where Claude looks

No source = it guesses. ✗ "Summarize my day." ✓ "Scan my Slack, Gmail, and Calendar"

⏰ TIMEFRAME — the boundary

No boundary = old items bury today's. ✗ (no time limit) ✓ "since yesterday 5 PM"

🗂️ GROUPING — who sorts

A flat list means you still do the sorting. ✗ (one long list) ✓ "group into needs-action vs FYI-only"

🎯 CONSTRAINT — force a decision

Ten items is a list; three is a decision. ✗ "…tell me what's important." ✓ "give me my top 3 priorities"

🚫 EXCLUSION — what NOT to include

Telling Claude what NOT to do is half the power. ✗ (handled items come back too) ✓ "skip anything I've already replied to"

⚖ SCORING — RANGE + SPEED TIER

RANGE scores your prompt /25 (0–5 per dimension). ≤40% = 1x SPEED · 41–75% = 5x SPEED · 76%+ = 10x SPEED → 🔓 DAY 2 UNLOCKED
